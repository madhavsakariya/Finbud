BANKING AND SAVINGS IN INDIA

Savings Account Interest Rates:
- Most banks offer 2.5% to 4% per year on savings accounts
- Small finance banks offer up to 7% on savings accounts
- Interest above Rs 10,000 per year is taxable

RBI Guidelines:
- RBI (Reserve Bank of India) is the central bank of India
- Repo rate: Rate at which RBI lends to banks (affects home loan and FD rates)
- When repo rate increases, loan EMIs increase and FD rates improve
- When repo rate decreases, loan EMIs decrease and FD rates fall

DICGC Insurance:
- All bank deposits insured up to Rs 5,00,000 per bank per depositor
- Covers savings, FD, RD, current accounts
- Provided by Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation

Home Loans:
- Interest rates: 8.5% to 10.5% per year currently
- Maximum tenure: 30 years
- Tax benefit: Principal repayment under 80C (up to Rs 1.5 lakh), interest under Section 24 (up to Rs 2 lakh)
- PMAY subsidy available for first time home buyers under certain income criteria

Personal Loans:
- Interest rates: 10% to 24% per year
- No collateral required
- Tenure: 1 to 5 years
- Avoid for investments, use only for emergencies

Credit Cards:
- Pay full outstanding every month to avoid interest
- Interest on unpaid balance: 36% to 48% per year (very high)
- Credit score (CIBIL) affected by payment history
- Ideal credit utilization: below 30% of credit limit

Emergency Fund:
- Keep 3 to 6 months of expenses in liquid, safe investments
- Best options: Savings account, liquid mutual funds, short term FD
- Never invest emergency fund in stocks or equity mutual funds

CIBIL Score:
- Credit score ranging from 300 to 900
- Above 750: Excellent, easy loan approval at best rates
- 650 to 750: Good, most loans approved
- Below 650: Poor, loan rejection likely or high interest rates
- Improve by: Paying EMIs on time, keeping credit utilization low, not applying for multiple loans at once

UPI and Digital Payments:
- UPI limit: Rs 1,00,000 per transaction (Rs 2,00,000 for certain categories)
- IMPS: Instant transfer up to Rs 5,00,000
- NEFT: Settled in batches, no limit
- RTGS: For amounts above Rs 2,00,000, real time settlement
