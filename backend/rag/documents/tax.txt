INCOME TAX INDIA 2024-25

New Tax Regime Slabs:
- Up to Rs 3,00,000: No tax
- Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 6,00,000: 5%
- Rs 6,00,001 to Rs 9,00,000: 10%
- Rs 9,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 15%
- Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 15,00,000: 20%
- Above Rs 15,00,000: 30%

Old Tax Regime Slabs:
- Up to Rs 2,50,000: No tax
- Rs 2,50,001 to Rs 5,00,000: 5%
- Rs 5,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 20%
- Above Rs 10,00,000: 30%

Standard Deduction:
- Rs 50,000 for salaried individuals in both regimes.

Section 80C Deductions (Old Regime only):
- Maximum limit: Rs 1,50,000 per year
- Eligible investments: PPF, ELSS, NSC, LIC premium, EPF, home loan principal, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, 5-year FD

Section 80D - Health Insurance:
- Self and family: up to Rs 25,000
- Senior citizen parents: additional Rs 50,000
- Maximum total: Rs 75,000

TDS on Fixed Deposit Interest:
- TDS deducted if interest exceeds Rs 40,000/year (Rs 50,000 for senior citizens)
- TDS rate: 10% if PAN provided, 20% if PAN not provided
- Submit Form 15G (below 60 years) or 15H (senior citizens) to avoid TDS if total income is below taxable limit

Capital Gains Tax:
- Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) on equity: 15% if held less than 1 year
- Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) on equity: 10% on gains above Rs 1,00,000 if held more than 1 year
- Debt fund gains taxed as per income tax slab

HRA Exemption:
- Least of the following is exempt:
  1. Actual HRA received
  2. 50% of salary (metro cities) or 40% (non-metro)
  3. Actual rent paid minus 10% of salary
