MUTUAL FUNDS IN INDIA

Types of Mutual Funds:
- Equity Funds: Invest in stocks. High risk, high return. Best for 5+ year goals.
- Debt Funds: Invest in bonds and government securities. Low risk, stable returns. Best for 1-3 year goals.
- Hybrid Funds: Mix of equity and debt. Medium risk. Good for 3-5 year goals.
- Index Funds: Track Nifty 50 or Sensex. Low cost, passive. Good for long term.
- Liquid Funds: Very short term debt funds. Safe alternative to savings account for emergency fund.

SIP - Systematic Investment Plan:
- Invest fixed amount every month in a mutual fund
- Minimum SIP amount: Rs 500/month in most funds
- Benefits: Rupee cost averaging, disciplined investing, no need to time market
- Best for: Long term wealth creation, retirement planning

ELSS - Equity Linked Savings Scheme:
- Tax saving mutual fund under Section 80C
- Lock-in period: 3 years (shortest among all 80C options)
- Returns: Market linked, historically 12-15% per year
- Risk: High (equity market linked)

PPF - Public Provident Fund:
- Government backed, completely safe
- Current interest rate: 7.1% per year (compounded annually)
- Lock-in: 15 years (partial withdrawal allowed after 7 years)
- Tax: EEE status (Exempt at investment, Exempt on interest, Exempt on maturity)
- Maximum investment: Rs 1,50,000 per year
- Minimum investment: Rs 500 per year

NPS - National Pension System:
- Retirement savings scheme by government
- Additional tax deduction: Rs 50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B) over and above 80C limit
- Returns: Market linked, mix of equity and debt
- Withdrawal: 60% lump sum at retirement, 40% must be used to buy annuity

Fixed Deposits:
- Safe, guaranteed returns
- Interest rates: 6.5% to 8% depending on bank and tenure
- Senior citizens get 0.25% to 0.5% extra interest
- TDS applicable on interest above Rs 40,000/year

Stocks and Equity:
- Listed on BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange) and NSE (National Stock Exchange)
- Nifty 50: Top 50 companies by market cap on NSE
- Sensex: Top 30 companies on BSE
- Demat account required to invest in stocks
- Long term investing recommended over trading for beginners
